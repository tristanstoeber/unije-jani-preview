Welcome – it will be our great pleasure to have you as our guest!

When you retreat from the noise and stress of city life, you can find your little place of peace on the green island of Unije: clean sea and warm sun on beautiful beaches smelling of salt, rosemary and lavender, with concerts of cicadas and seagulls smoothly accompanied by the sounds of the sea …

Our apartments are 100 m from the beach!

Island Unije

Unije (17 km²) is an oasis of peace – no cars! – with beautiful old village architecture. It lies in the Kvarner Bay.

Getting here

  • Catamaran Rijeka – Mali Lošinj
  • Catamaran Pula – Zadar
  • Daily boat Unije – Susak – Mali Lošinj

Events

Lignjada – a squid-fishing and darts competition with a folk feast – takes place around the weekend of the feast of St. Andrew (30 Nov). Depending on the weather it can be a week earlier or later.
